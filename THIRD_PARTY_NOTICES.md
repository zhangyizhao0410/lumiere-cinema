## Externally loaded assets

- The movie catalog links to Sintel, Tears of Steel and Elephants Dream videos and stills in the Google public video sample bucket. These are not stored in this repository. Film attribution, source and license links are shown in each film's details.
- Illustrative photographs load from Unsplash. These are mood images, not official movie posters.
- Noto Sans SC loads from Google Fonts; the site falls back to system fonts when it is unavailable.
- Other movie entries contain catalog information only. Their copyrighted films are not included.
